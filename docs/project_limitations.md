# CogniStream Project Limitations

## Current Limitations

1. Analysis quality depends on the accuracy and completeness of the available dataset.
2. Missing or inconsistent records may affect analytical results.
3. RAG responses depend on the relevance and quality of retrieved documents.
4. AI-generated responses may require manual verification.
5. Correlation between variables does not establish causation.
6. Dashboard results depend on the selected filters and available data.

## Future Scope

- Improve data validation and preprocessing.
- Expand the document knowledge base.
- Improve retrieval relevance.
- Add more analytical visualizations.
- Expand testing coverage.
- Improve dashboard usability.

## Conclusion

These limitations should be considered when interpreting results and planning future improvements.
